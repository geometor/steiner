2. Comparison with Classical Compass-and-Straightedge¶

In classical geometry (euclid-elements), drawing a parallel requires Euclid I.31 (compass arcs to copy an angle, \(4R_1 + 2R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3, S = 13\)). Steiner’s parallel requires 4 additional ruler alignments (\(S = 17\)), but completely eliminates all 3 compass arcs (\(m_1 = m_2 = m_3 = 0\)).
