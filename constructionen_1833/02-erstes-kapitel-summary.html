Summary: Projective Invariants, Complete Quadrangles & Circular Polars¶

Quantitative Operations & Harmonic Formulas

1. Harmonic Cross-Ratio & Complete Quadrangle¶

For four collinear points \(A, B, C, D\), the cross-ratio satisfies:

\[(A, B; C, D) = \frac{\overrightarrow{AC}}{\overrightarrow{BC}} : \frac{\overrightarrow{AD}}{\overrightarrow{BD}} = -1 \iff \frac{2}{AB} = \frac{1}{AC} + \frac{1}{AD} \quad (\text{when } A \text{ is origin}).\]

Executing this construction via complete quadrangle requires:

\[\mathrm{Op.}_{\text{Harmonic}} : 9R_1 + 6R_2, \qquad S = 15, \quad E = 9, \quad N_{\text{circles}} = 0.\]

2. Polar of a Point with Respect to a Circle¶

Constructing the polar \(p\) of pole \(P\) via an inscribed complete quadrangle on two secants:

\[\mathrm{Op.}_{\text{Polar}} : 12R_1 + 7R_2, \qquad S = 19, \quad E = 12, \quad N_{\text{circles}} = 0.\]
