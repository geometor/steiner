Geometrical Constructions¶

Die geometrischen Constructionen, ausgeführt mittelst der geraden Linie und Eines festen Kreises, als Lehrgegenstand auf höheren Unterrichts-Anstalten und zur praktischen Benutzung — Jakob Steiner (Berlin, 1833)

Author: Jakob Steiner
First Edition (Berlin, 1833): Ferdinand Dümmler (110 S., 2 Kupfertafeln, Fig. 1–25).
Ostwald’s Klassiker Edition (Leipzig, 1895): Nr. 60, edited by A. J. von Oettingen (Wilhelm Engelmann).
English Translation: Scholarly edition curated for the GEOMETOR Digital Scriptorium.
Synchronized 4-Track Codex: English · German · Summary · Notes, paired with exact symbolic geometry in geometor.steiner.constructions.

“All geometric problems of elementary geometry can be solved using only the straightedge, provided that a single fixed auxiliary circle together with its center is drawn in the plane.”

—Jakob Steiner, Die geometrischen Constructionen, § 1 (p. 3)

Table of Contents¶

Introductory Survey: Reduction of Compass Operations to the Straightedge (§§ 1–2, pp. 3–6 / [1]–[6])

English · German · Summary · Notes

Foundational statement of the Poncelet–Steiner theorem, survey of the eight fundamental tasks, and historical lineage from Mascheroni, Poncelet, Lambert, and Brianchon.

Chapter I: Harmonic Pencils and Points, Complete Quadrangle, and Polars (§§ 3–10, pp. 7–28 / [7]–[28])

English · German · Summary · Notes

Harmonic pencils, cross-ratio invariance \((A, B; C, D) = -1\), straightedge construction of fourth harmonic points via the complete quadrangle (\(S = 15\)), and pole-polar involution on drawn circles (\(S = 19\)).

Chapter II: Centers of Similitude and Radical Axis of Circles (§§ 11–17, pp. 29–50 / [29]–[67])

English · German · Summary · Notes

External and internal centers of similitude (Aehnlichkeitspunkte) \(A, I\), the four axes of similitude of three circles, coining of the power of a point \(d^2 - R^2\), and the straightedge construction of the radical axis.

Chapter III: First through Fifth Fundamental Problems (§ 18, pp. 50–56 / [68]–[76])

English · German · Summary · Notes

The linear and angular straightedge constructions: drawing parallels via diameter \(ab\) (\(S = 17\)), segment multiplication and division, dropping perpendiculars via Thales chords and orthocenters (\(S = 15\)), laying off angles, and angle bisection.

Chapter III (Continued): Sixth through Eighth Fundamental Problems (§ 18, pp. 56–62 / [76]–[85])

English · German · Summary · Notes

Transfer of segments via similitude centers, intersection of a straight line with an un-drawn circle via homothetic projection onto \(\Omega\) (\(S = 25\)), intersection of two un-drawn circles via radical axis (\(S = 42\)), and proof of the indispensability of center \(M\).
