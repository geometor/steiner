1. Instrument Regimes & Operation Subspaces¶

Constructibility & Operation Subspaces Across Classical and Minimal Instruments¶

Regime

Primary Literature

Operation Symbol

Algebraic Field & Invariance

Canonical Ruler + Compass

Euclid (euclid-elements), Lemoine (lemoine-1902)

\(l_1 R_1 + l_2 R_2 + m_1 C_1 + m_2 C_2 + m_3 C_3\)

Full Euclidean quadratic closure \(\mathbb{E}\) (nested square roots \(\sqrt{x^2 \pm y^2}\))

Mascheroni Compass Alone

Mohr (mohr-1672), Mascheroni (mascheroni-1797)

\(m_1 C_1 + m_2 C_2 + m_3 C_3\) (\(l_1 = l_2 = 0\))

Full Euclidean quadratic closure \(\mathbb{E}\) (straight lines implicit as point-pairs \((A, B)\))

Poncelet–Steiner Straightedge + Fixed Circle :math:`M`

Poncelet (poncelet-1822), Steiner (steiner-1833)

\(l_1 R_1 + l_2 R_2\) (\(m_1 = m_2 = m_3 = 0\))

Full Euclidean quadratic closure \(\mathbb{E}\) (circles implicit as center \(M_1\) + radius endpoint)

Hilbert Straightedge + Eichmaß (Unit Gauge)

Hilbert (hilbert-1899, §§ 36–39)

\(R_1, R_2\) + movable unit-segment transferrer

Strictly Pythagorean subfield \(\Omega_{\text{pyth}}\) (\(\sqrt{1 + \omega^2}\), strictly smaller than \(\mathbb{E}\))
