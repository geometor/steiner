§ 13: The Four Axes of Similitude of Three Circles¶

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Constructionen (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0045, Fig. 16 (1895, p. 41)

  1. Given three coplanar circles whose centers M1, M2, M3 are not collinear, each pair has an external and an internal center of similitude: A3, I3 for \((M_1, M_2)\), A2, I2 for \((M_1, M_3)\), and A1, I1 for \((M_2, M_3)\).

Theorem (§ 13): These six centers of similitude lie three by three along four straight lines—the axes of similitude:

  1. One external axis of similitude: A3 A2 A1.

  2. Three mixed axes of similitude: A3 I2 I1, A2 I1 I3, and A1 I2 I3.

Because parallel diameters can be drawn once a fixed circle is given, all six centers and four axes of similitude are straightedge-constructible!
