§ 14: Locus of Equal Difference of Squared Distances¶

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Constructionen (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

Chapter II — Part III: Power of Circles. A. Locus of Equal Powers.

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0048, Fig. 17 (1895, p. 44)

Given two fixed points M and M1 in the plane, what is the locus of all points N for which the difference of the squares of their distances from \(M\) and \(M_1\) equals a given constant \(u^2\)?

\[MN^2 - M_1 N^2 = u^2.\]

Dropping the perpendicular NQ onto the line MM1, the Pythagorean theorem in right triangles \(\triangle NQM\) and \(\triangle NQM_1\) yields:

\[NM^2 - QM^2 = NQ^2 = NM_1^2 - QM_1^2 \implies QM^2 - QM_1^2 = MN^2 - M_1 N^2 = u^2.\]

Because there is a unique point Q on line \(MM_1\) satisfying \(QM^2 - QM_1^2 = u^2\), the locus of N is the fixed straight line NQ perpendicular to MM1.
