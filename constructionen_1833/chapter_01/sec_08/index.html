§ 8: Constructions from a Parallelogram or Two Rational Ratios¶

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Constructionen (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

Part II.B — When Two Pairs of Parallels (a Parallelogram) or Two Rationally Divided Segments Are Given.

  1. Given any parallelogram :g:`ABCD <0022.g1:< A B C D >>` in the plane, with diagonals :g:`AC <0022.g1:/ A C />` and :g:`BD <0022.g1:/ B D />` meeting at :g:`E <0022.g1:E>`, one can draw with the straightedge alone: (a) a line parallel to any given line in any direction through any given point, and (b) any rational multiple or subdivision of any segment.

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0022, Fig. 7 (1895, p. 18)

Indeed, drawing EF through E parallel to ADBC creates three equidistant parallels (ADEFBC) which bisect any non-parallel transversal GK at F, immediately enabling parallels to GK via § 6, I!

  1. The exact same universal affine power holds whenever any of the following four data are given: (a) three parallels cutting a fourth line in a given rational ratio (Fig. 8), (b) two parallels bearing two segments in a given rational ratio (Fig. 8), (c) two parallels and one divided segment (Fig. 9), or (d) two non-parallel divided segments (Fig. 10).

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0023, Fig. 8 (1895, p. 19)
