Seventh Problem (§ 18, [78]–[82]): Intersecting a Line with an Un-Drawn Circle¶

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Constructionen (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« To find the mutual intersection points of a given straight line and a given (un-drawn, virtual) circle :math:`(M_1, r_1)`. »

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0062, Fig. 23 (1895, p. 58)

Let g1 h1 be the given line, and (M1, a1) the un-drawn circle defined by its center M1 and radius segment M1 a1.

  1. Construct the center of similitude A (or I) of fixed circle M and virtual circle \((M_1, r_1)\) via parallel radii Ma and M1 a1.

  2. Homothetically project line g1 h1 from center A onto its corresponding line gh relative to the actually drawn fixed circle M, where \(gh \parallel g_1 h_1\).

  3. Line gh intersects the drawn circumference of \(\Omega\) in two visible points g and h.

  4. Draw straightedge rays Ag and Ah: their intersections with the given line are the exact intersection points g1 and h1 of the un-drawn circle with the line!
