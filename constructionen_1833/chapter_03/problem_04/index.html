Fourth Problem (§ 18, [75]): Laying Off an Angle Equal to a Given Angle¶

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Constructionen (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« Through a given point, to draw a straight line making with a given straight line an angle equal to a given angle. »

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0059, Fig. 21 (1895, p. 55)

Draw diameters ab and cd of the fixed circle M parallel (by Problem 1) to the two arms mA and mC of the given angle, transporting the angle directly to center \(M\) as central angle \(\angle aMc = \angle AmC\). Drawing a parallel through the target point completes the transfer onto any target line pq.
