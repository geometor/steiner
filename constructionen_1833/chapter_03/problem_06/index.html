Sixth Problem (§ 18, [76]–[78]): Transferring a Given Segment¶

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Constructionen (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« From a given point along a given ray, to lay off a segment equal in length to a given segment. »

▸ Archival Plate — Folio 0060, Fig. 22 (1895, p. 56)

Given a segment M1 a1, we regard it as the radius of an un-drawn (virtual) circle \((M_1, r_1)\). To lay off this exact length from a new point M2 along any ray:

  1. Through center M of the fixed circle (Ma), draw diameter ab parallel to M1 a1 (by Problem 1).

  2. Line \(ba_1\) intersects MM1 at the internal center of similitude I1 (or \(aa_1\) gives the external center \(A_1\)).

  3. Transferring center \(M_1\) to target M2 along equal parallel radii via I2 projects radius \(R\) back onto any desired direction around M2 in exact equal length \(M_1 a_1\), using straightedge alone!
