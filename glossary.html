Glossary¶

Synthetic & Constructive Geometry Terminology (Deutsch · English · Français)

Fester Hülfskreis (mit gegebenem Mittelpunct M) (German · Fixed Auxiliary Circle with Given Center M)

Steiner’s single permanent reference circle in the plane. Together with its center \(M\), it supplies an involution of parallel diameters and orthogonal chords enabling pure straightedge simulation of all compass intersections; without \(M\), projective collineations fixing the circle break Euclidean metric structure.

Aehnlichkeitspunkte (A, I) (German · External and Internal Centers of Similitude (Homothetic Centers))

In Steiner’s Zweites Kapitel (§§ 12–13) and Sechste bis Achte Aufgabe (§ 18), the harmonic centers \(A\) and \(I\) on the line of centers \(MM_1\) through which parallel radii of the fixed drawn circle \(M\) and an un-drawn virtual circle \(M_1\) correspond under straightedge projection.

Aehnlichkeitsaxe (German · Axis of Similitude)

A straight line passing through three centers of similitude of three circles taken in pairs; Steiner demonstrates that the six centers of similitude lie three by three on four axes of similitude by straightedge alone.

Linie der gleichen Potenzen (Axe radicale) (German · Line of Equal Powers (Radical Axis))

Coined via Steiner’s “Potenz des Punctes” (1826/1833, §§ 14–17) and Gaultier/Chasles’s “axe radical”: the straight locus of points having equal power \(d_1^2 - r_1^2 = d_2^2 - r_2^2\) with respect to two circles, constructed by straightedge alone in Problem 8 to intersect two un-drawn circles.

Potenz eines Punktes (German · Power of a Point with Respect to a Circle)

For a point \(P\) and circle \((M, r)\) at distance \(d = PM\), the product of secant segments \(PA \cdot PB = d^2 - r^2\); introduced by Steiner in 1826 and fundamental to his theory of radical axes.

Punkt der gleichen Potenzen (Radikalzentrum) (German · Point of Equal Powers (Radical Center))

The unique intersection point of the three radical axes of three circles taken in pairs, from which tangents to all three circles have equal length.

Harmonische Strahlen (Harmonisches Strahlenbündel) (German · Harmonic Rays (Harmonic Pencil))

Four concurrent straight lines \(a, b, c, d\) whose cross-ratio \((a, b; c, d) = -1\); every transversal cutting the four rays produces a harmonic range of four collinear points.

Harmonische Punkte (Harmonische Teilung) (German · Harmonic Points (Harmonic Division))

Four collinear points \(A, B, C, D\) such that the cross-ratio \((A, B; C, D) = (CA/CB) / (DA/DB) = -1\); \(C\) and \(D\) divide the segment \(AB\) internally and externally in the same ratio.

Vollständiges Viereck (German · Complete Quadrangle)

A configuration of four points (vertices), no three collinear, and the six straight lines joining them; its three diagonal points form a diagonal triangle whose sides divide the opposite vertices harmonically.

Vollständiges Vierseit (German · Complete Quadrilateral)

A configuration of four straight lines (sides), no three concurrent, and their six intersection points; dual to the complete quadrangle, its three diagonals form a triangle whose vertices separate the sides harmonically.

Pol und Polare am Kreise (German · Pole and Polar with Respect to a Circle)

The projective relationship where each point \(P\) (pole) is paired with a straight line \(p\) (polar) such that any secant through \(P\) cutting the circle at \(A, B\) has its harmonic conjugate on \(p\); Steiner proves that polars can be constructed by straightedge alone.

Satz des Thales (German · Thales’ Theorem)

The theorem that an angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle; used by Steiner in the Dritte Aufgabe to construct perpendiculars using chords of the fixed circle.

Höhenschnittpunkt (Orthocentrum) (German · Orthocenter)

The common point of concurrency of the three altitudes of a triangle; constructed with straightedge alone on a diameter of the fixed circle to yield exact right angles.

Géométrographie Steinerienne (French · Steinerian Geometrography)

Quantitative operational analysis of constructions executed using only the straightedge (\(l_1 R_1 + l_2 R_2, \text{circles} = 0\)) with a single fixed circle and its center in the plane.

Géométrographie Mascheronienne (French · Mascheronian Geometrography)

Evaluation of constructions restricted exclusively to the compass (\(m_1 C_1 + m_2 C_2 + m_3 C_3, \text{lines} = 0\)) following Lorenzo Mascheroni (1797).

Eichmaß (Streckenüberträger) (German · Segment Transferrer / Unit Gauge)

In Hilbert’s Grundlagen der Geometrie (§ 36), an instrument capable of laying off a fixed unit segment along any ray; Hilbert proved that straightedge + Eichmaß generates only the pythagorean field, whereas Steiner’s drawn circle generates the full Euclidean closure.

Steiner-Punkt (German · Steiner Point)

In triangle geometry, the circumcircle point \(X(99)\) discovered by Steiner; in projective geometry, one of the 20 points where three Pascal lines concur, or the two star points \(S_+, S_-\) in the Pappus \(S_3\) configuration.

Équerre (Géométrographie de l’équerre) (French · Draftsman’s Set-Square / Right Triangle)

Auxiliary drafting triangle slid along a fixed straightedge to draw parallels and perpendiculars directly; first analyzed in Lemoine’s 1888 Oran note and formalized in Scientia No. 18 (1902) via elementary alignment and sliding operations.
