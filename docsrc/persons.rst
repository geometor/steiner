.. _persons:

======
People
======

.. rubric:: Primary Authors, Editors & Interlocutors in the Steiner Tradition

.. _person-steiner:

Jakob Steiner (1796–1863)
-------------------------

Swiss-born synthetic projective geometer; professor of geometry at the University of Berlin. Following *Systematische Entwickelung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten* (1832), Steiner published *Die geometrischen Constructionen, ausgeführt mittelst der geraden Linie und Eines festen Kreises* (1833), proving via harmonic ranges, centers of similitude, and radical axes that a single fixed circle drawn with its center suffices to execute all Euclidean constructions by straightedge alone.

.. _person-poncelet:

Jean-Victor Poncelet (1788–1867)
--------------------------------

French military engineer and founder of modern projective geometry. In his *Traité des propriétés projectives des figures* (1822, §§ 351–358), first stated that all ruler-and-compass problems can be solved by straightedge alone when a single circle and its center are given in the plane—a theorem rigorously carried out and organized into eight canonical problems by Jakob Steiner in 1833.

.. _person-mascheroni:

Lorenzo Mascheroni (1750–1800)
------------------------------

Italian mathematician and poet; professor of algebra and geometry at the University of Pavia. Author of *Adnotationes ad calculum integralem Euleri* (1790) and *La Geometria del Compasso* (Pavia, 1797), proving constructively across twelve books that every ruler-and-compass problem solvable by point determinations can be executed with the compass alone.

.. _person-mohr:

Georg Mohr (1640–1697)
----------------------

Danish mathematician; author of *Euclides Danicus* (1672). Published *Euclides Danicus* in Dutch and Danish at Amsterdam in 1672—125 years before Mascheroni—proving that all constructions of the first six books of Euclid can be carried out with a compass alone; rediscovered by Johannes Hjelmslev in 1928.

.. _person-lambert:

Johann Heinrich Lambert (1728–1777)
-----------------------------------

Swiss-German mathematician, physicist, and philosopher. In *Freie Perspective* (Zürich, 1759; 2nd ed. 1774), investigated perspective drawing with an ungraduated ruler alone, discovering that parallels and harmonic divisions can be constructed when certain fixed figures are present in the plane.

.. _person-brianchon:

Charles Julien Brianchon (1783–1864)
------------------------------------

French mathematician and artillery officer; author of Brianchon's Theorem (1806). Developed projective transversal theory in *Application de la théorie des transversales* (1817), providing straightedge constructions of polars and conic tangencies cited by Steiner in § 2.

.. _person-hilbert:

David Hilbert (1862–1943)
-------------------------

German mathematician; author of *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (1899). In Chapter VII (§§ 36–39) of *Grundlagen der Geometrie*, complemented Steiner's fixed-circle ruler constructions with algebraic criteria characterizing exactly which problems are solvable with straightedge and segment-transferrer (*Eichmaß*) alone, showing that *Eichmaß* yields only the pythagorean field whereas Steiner's drawn circle yields the full Euclidean quadratic closure.

.. _person-adler:

August Adler (1863–1923)
------------------------

Czech-Austrian geometer; author of *Theorie der geometrischen Konstruktionen* (1906). In *Zur Theorie der Mascheronischen Constructionen* (1890) and *Theorie der geometrischen Konstruktionen* (1906), gave systematic expositions linking Mascheroni's compass-only geometry, Steiner's fixed-circle constructions, and Lemoine's Géométrographie.

.. _person-lemoine:

Émile Michel Hyacinthe Lemoine (1840–1912)
------------------------------------------

French geometer and creator of Géométrographie. In *Scientia* No. 18 (1902, pp. 74–85), formalized "Géométrographie Steinerienne", quantitatively measuring Steiner's straightedge constructions with operation symbols :math:`l_1 R_1 + l_2 R_2`.

.. _person-oettingen:

Arthur Joachim von Oettingen (1836–1920)
----------------------------------------

German physicist and editor of Ostwald's Klassiker der exakten Wissenschaften. Edited and annotated the critical 1895 Leipzig edition of Steiner's *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60), preserving the 1833 Dümmler pagination ``[1]``–``[85]``.

.. _person-grueson:

Johann Philipp de Gruson (1768–1857)
------------------------------------

German mathematician and translator of Mascheroni. Professor of mathematics at the University of Berlin; published the German translation *Des Herrn Abt L. Mascheroni Geometrie des Zirkels* (Berlin, 1825), through which Steiner studied Mascheroni's methods.

.. _person-pappus:

Pappus of Alexandria (c. 290–c. 350 CE)
---------------------------------------

Late antique Greek geometer; author of the *Synagoge* (*Mathematical Collection*). Preserved the Treasury of Analysis in Book VII of the *Synagoge*, establishing the complete-quadrilateral harmonic division, projective collineations, and the Pappus hexagon theorem whose six-permutation family generates the two Steiner star points :math:`S_+, S_-`.

.. _person-pascal:

Blaise Pascal (1623–1662)
-------------------------

French mathematician, physicist, and religious philosopher. Author of the *Essay pour les coniques* (1640) formulating the Mystic Hexagram theorem; Steiner investigated the 60 Pascal lines and discovered the 20 Steiner points where three Pascal lines concur.

.. _person-euclid:

Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BCE)
-------------------------------------

Alexandrian mathematician; author of the *Elements*. Codified classical deductive geometry from straightedge and compass postulates (Postulates 1–3); Steiner proved that Postulate 3 (drawing arbitrary circles) is completely redundant if a single fixed circle and its center are given.
