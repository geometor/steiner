Georg Mohr
==========

Danish mathematician; author of *Euclides Danicus* (1672). Published *Euclides Danicus* in Dutch and Danish at Amsterdam in 1672—125 years before Mascheroni—proving that all constructions of the first six books of Euclid can be carried out with a compass alone; rediscovered by Johannes Hjelmslev in 1928.
