Appendix Problem 4: Contact Point Given Four Points and One Tangent
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When four points and one tangent of a conic section are given, to find the point in which the tangent touches the conic.* »

Let :math:`A` be the given tangent and :math:`\mathfrak{A}, \mathfrak{B}, \mathfrak{C}, \mathfrak{D}` the four points (pp. 69–70). The three pairs of opposite sides of the complete quadrangle (:math:`\mathfrak{AB}, \mathfrak{CD}`; :math:`\mathfrak{AC}, \mathfrak{BD}`; :math:`\mathfrak{AD}, \mathfrak{BC}`) cut line :math:`A` in three involution pairs :math:`(a, \alpha), (b, \beta), (c, \gamma)`. The fixed circle :math:`\Omega` determines the two double points :math:`\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{s}` of the involution, yielding two possible conics. By the second method (p. 70), two pairs of opposite sides—giving :math:`\mathfrak{p}` and the harmonic axis :math:`\mathfrak{t}`—already suffice.
