Appendix Problem 6: Contact Points Given Three Points and Two Tangents
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When three points and two tangents of a conic section are given, to find the points in which the conic touches the tangents.* »

Given points :math:`\mathfrak{A}, \mathfrak{B}, \mathfrak{C}` and tangents :math:`A, B` (pp. 70–71 / ``[98]``–``[99]``), the three secant chords join point-pairs onto :math:`A` and :math:`B`, producing two pairs of involution double points :math:`\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{s}` on :math:`\mathfrak{AB}` and :math:`\mathfrak{r}_1, \mathfrak{s}_1` on :math:`\mathfrak{AC}`. Pairing the four resulting secants (:math:`u, x, y, z` intersecting :math:`A, B`) produces in general **four** conic solutions.
