Appendix Problem 18: Common Points/Tangents from 2 Shared Points & 3 Tangents Each
==================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When two conics have two common points given, and in addition any three tangents of each are given, to find their remaining common points and common tangents.* »

Because each conic is specified by two points and three tangents (Problem 7, yielding 4 conics each), Problem 18 admits in general :math:`4 \times 4 = 16` solution combinations, each resolved directly by **Problem 16** (p. 76 / ``[105]``).
