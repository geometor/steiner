Appendix Problem 22: Plotting a Circle in the Field Around an Inaccessible Center
=================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *In the field, when the center* :math:`M_1` *and one point* :math:`a_1` *on the circumference of a circle are given, yet an obstacle* :math:`RS` *separates them so that the radius cannot be laid off continuously across the terrain, to determine arbitrarily many other points* :math:`c_1, d_1, \dots` *of that circle using only surveying staves and a measuring chain.* »

.. picture:: 0082.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 25 — Surveying a circle around obstacle RS using staves a₁, m, n and auxiliary circle M (§ 20, Problem 22)
   :caption: Fig. 25 — Inaccessible center M₁, point a₁, collinear stakes a₁, m, n, obstacle RS, and auxiliary circle M constructing c₁, d₁ (§ 20, Problem 22)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0082, Fig. 25 (1895, p. 78)

   .. picture:: 0082.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 25 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 78)

1. Extend the visible segment beyond :g:`a_1 <0082.g1:a1>` by aligning two equal chain lengths :g:`a_1 m <0082.g1:m>` :math:`=` :g:`mn <0082.g1:n>` (pp. 78–80 / ``[108]``–``[110]``). On clear ground around a point :g:`M <0082.g1:M>`, sweep a fixed auxiliary circle :g:`M <0082.g1:( M a )>` using the chain as radius, with diameter :g:`ab <0082.g1:/ a b />` parallel to :g:`M_1 a_1 <0082.g1:/ M1 n />`.
2. Mark external and internal similitude stakes :math:`A` (meeting :g:`a_1 a <0082.g1:a>`) and :math:`I` (meeting :g:`a_1 b <0082.g1:b>`) along the line of centers :g:`M_1 M <0082.g1:/ M M1 />`.
3. For any diameter :math:`cd` traced by the chain around :g:`M <0082.g1:M>`, the sightlines :math:`Ac \cap Id` and :math:`Ad \cap Ic` give exact target points :g:`c_1, d_1 <0082.g1:c1>` on the blocked circle! (Pp. 79–80 extend the method even if three circumcircle points :math:`a_1, d_1, e_1` replace :math:`M_1, a_1`.)
