Editorial Annotations by A. J. von Oettingen (Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60, 1895)
===============================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Editorial Annotations (*Anmerkungen*), Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (Leipzig, 1895, pp. 81–84 / folios ``0085``–``0088``), followed by *Inhalt* (p. 85, folio ``0089``) and the 1833 Plates.**

Editor :person:`Arthur Joachim von Oettingen <oettingen>` supplied ten numbered editorial notes (Nos. 1–10) clarifying textual conventions, historical background, and alternative algebraic or geometric proofs across the 1833 edition:

* **Archival Proof Notes**: Clarifying the harmonic-division criteria (§§ 3–6), Steiner's cross-ratio ratios, and the geometric steps in Chapter I.
* **Euler Line & Nine-Point Circle Note (Note 4, ad p. 37 / Fig. 15)**: Tracing the collineation of orthocenter :math:`H`, centroid :math:`I`, circumcenter :math:`M`, and nine-point center :math:`M_1` back to Euler, Brianchon–Poncelet (1821), and Feuerbach (1822).
* **Pagination Preservation**: Bracketed bold numbers ``[1]``–``[110]`` embedded throughout the 1895 text mark the exact page boundaries of Steiner's original 1833 Berlin edition (Ferdinand Dümmler).
