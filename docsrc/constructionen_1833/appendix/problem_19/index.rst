Appendix Problem 19: Common Tangents/Points from 2 Shared Tangents & 3 Points Each
==================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When two conics have two common tangents given, and in addition any three points of each are given, to find their remaining common tangents and common points.* »

Dual of Problem 18: each conic yields up to 4 candidates via **Problem 6**, giving up to 16 cases resolved directly by **Problem 17**.
