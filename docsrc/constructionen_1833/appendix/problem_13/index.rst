Appendix Problem 13: Tangents from Point to Conic from 4 Tangents + 1 Contact Point
===================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the straight lines through a given point touching a conic given by (a) four tangents together with the contact point of one of them, or (b) four points together with the tangent at one of them.* »

Dual of Problem 12: five linear conditions uniquely specify the conic, allowing direct tangent construction via **Problem 3**.
