Appendix Problem 12: Line Cuts with Conic from 4 Points + 1 Contact Tangent
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the mutual intersection points of a given straight line and a conic section given by (a) four points together with the tangent at one of them, or (b) four tangents together with the contact point of one of them.* »

Because the point of tangency counts as a *known* doubled element, only a single conic exists; it is uniquely resolved linearly and cut via **Problem 2**.
