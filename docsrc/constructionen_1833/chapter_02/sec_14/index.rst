§ 14: Locus of Equal Difference of Squared Distances
====================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Chapter II — Part III: Power of Circles. A. Locus of Equal Powers.**

.. picture:: 0048.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 17 — Locus line NQ perpendicular to MM1 where MN^2 - M1N^2 = u^2 (§ 14)
   :caption: Fig. 17 — Fixed points M, M1 and orthogonal locus NQ (§ 14)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0048, Fig. 17 (1895, p. 44)

   .. picture:: 0048.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 17 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 44)

Given two fixed points :g:`M <0048.g1:M>` and :g:`M_1 <0048.g1:M1>` in the plane, what is the locus of all points :g:`N <0048.g1:N>` for which the difference of the squares of their distances from :math:`M` and :math:`M_1` equals a given constant :math:`u^2`?

.. math::

   MN^2 - M_1 N^2 = u^2.

Dropping the perpendicular :g:`NQ <0048.g1:/ N Q />` onto the line :g:`MM_1 <0048.g1:/ M M1 />`, the Pythagorean theorem in right triangles :math:`\triangle NQM` and :math:`\triangle NQM_1` yields:

.. math::

   NM^2 - QM^2 = NQ^2 = NM_1^2 - QM_1^2 \implies QM^2 - QM_1^2 = MN^2 - M_1 N^2 = u^2.

Because there is a **unique** point :g:`Q <0048.g1:Q>` on line :math:`MM_1` satisfying :math:`QM^2 - QM_1^2 = u^2`, the locus of :g:`N` is the fixed straight line :g:`NQ <0048.g1:/ N Q />` **perpendicular** to :g:`MM_1 <0048.g1:/ M M1 />`.
