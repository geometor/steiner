§ 15: Power of a Point and the Line of Equal Powers (Radical Axis)
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

Setting :math:`u^2 = R^2 - R_1^2` in § 14 for two circles :math:`(M, R)` and :math:`(M_1, R_1)` yields the locus of points :math:`N` satisfying:

.. math::

   MN^2 - R^2 = M_1 N^2 - R_1^2.

Steiner names :math:`d^2 - R^2` the :term:`power of the point <potenz-eines-punktes>` with respect to circle :math:`(M, R)`, and calls the straight locus :math:`NQ \perp MM_1` the :term:`line of equal powers <linie-der-gleichen-potenzen>` (radical axis / *Linie der gleichen Tangenten*).

* If the two circles intersect in two real points, their radical axis is their common secant line.
* If the circles are tangent, it is their common tangent at the point of contact.
* From every external point :math:`N` on the radical axis, the tangents :math:`NB` and :math:`NB_1` to the two circles are **equal**: :math:`NB^2 = MN^2 - R^2 = M_1 N^2 - R_1^2 = NB_1^2`. From every internal point, the minimum chords bisected at :math:`N` are equal.
