§ 15: Notes — Steiner's 1826 Coining of Potenz des Punctes
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0048 (p. 44)

The concept of the :term:`power of a point <potenz-eines-punktes>` (*Potenz des Punctes*) was introduced by :person:`Jakob Steiner <steiner>` in Crelle's *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (Bd. 1, 1826, pp. 161–184), unifying Euclid III.35 and III.36 into one signed quadratic invariant.
