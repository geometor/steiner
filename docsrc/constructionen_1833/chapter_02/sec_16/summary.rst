§ 16: Summary — Concurrency of Radical Axes
===========================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0050 (p. 46)

* **Concurrency**: :math:`N_1 Q_1 \cap N_2 Q_2 \cap N_3 Q_3 = \{q\}` (whenever centers :math:`M_1, M_2, M_3` are non-collinear).
* **Equal Tangents**: :math:`qB_1 = qB_2 = qB_3` when :math:`q` is external to all three circles.
