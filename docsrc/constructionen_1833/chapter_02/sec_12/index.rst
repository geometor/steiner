§ 12: External and Internal Centers of Similitude of Two Circles
================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

   :alt: Figure 14 — External and internal centers of similitude A, I of two circles (§ 12)
   :caption: Fig. 14 — Two circles M, M1 with parallel diameters ab, a1 b1 defining harmonic centers A, I (§ 12)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0036, Fig. 14 (1895, p. 32)

   .. picture:: 0036.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 14 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 32)

I. Given any two coplanar circles :g:`M <0036.g1:( M a )>` and :g:`M_1 <0036.g1:( M1 a1 )>` with radii :math:`R` and :math:`r_1`, draw any two parallel diameters :g:`ab <0036.g1:/ a b />` and :g:`a_1 b_1 <0036.g1:/ a1 b1 />`. The lines joining endpoints on the same side of the line of centers :g:`MM_1 <0036.g1:/ M M1 />` (:g:`aa_1, bb_1 <0036.g1:/ A a1 />`) meet the axis in the **external center of similitude** :term:`A <aehnlichkeitspunkte>`, while cross-joins (:g:`ab_1, ba_1 <0036.g1:/ a b1 />`) meet the axis in the **internal center of similitude** :term:`I <aehnlichkeitspunkte>`:

.. math::

   \frac{\overrightarrow{AM}}{\overrightarrow{AM_1}} = \frac{R}{r_1}, \qquad \frac{\overrightarrow{IM}}{\overrightarrow{IM_1}} = -\frac{R}{r_1} \implies (M, M_1; A, I) = -1.

II–III. Every ray through :g:`A` or :g:`I` establishes a direct homothety mapping :math:`(M, R)` onto :math:`(M_1, r_1)`.

*Footnote Application (Fig. 15, pp. 37–40)*: Applying § 11 to triangle :g:`abc <0041.g1:< a b c >>` and its medial triangle :g:`a_1 b_1 c_1 <0041.g1:a1>` (ratio :math:`2 : 1` from centroid :g:`I <0041.g1:I>`), Steiner proves in a few lines that the circumcenter :g:`M <0041.g1:M>`, centroid :g:`I <0041.g1:I>`, orthocenter :g:`H <0041.g1:H>`, and nine-point center :g:`M_1 <0041.g1:M1>` are collinear along the **Euler line** with :math:`HI = 2 IM` and :math:`M_1` bisecting :g:`HM <0041.g1:/ H M />`!
