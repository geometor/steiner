§ 17: Notes — Bridge to Chapter III Problem 8
=============================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0051 (p. 47)

Section **§ 17** (pp. 47–50, folios ``0051``–``0054``) is the crowning theorem of Chapter II: Steiner uses it verbatim in **Chapter III, Problem 8** (repeating Fig. 18 on p. 61, folio ``0065``) to construct the radical axis of two un-drawn circles solely from straightedge joins!
