§ 13: The Four Axes of Similitude of Three Circles
==================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

.. picture:: 0045.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 16 — Four axes of similitude of three circles M1, M2, M3 (§ 13)
   :caption: Fig. 16 — Three circles M1, M2, M3 and their six centers of similitude A1, I1, A2, I2, A3, I3 lying three by three on four axes (§ 13)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0045, Fig. 16 (1895, p. 41)

   .. picture:: 0045.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 16 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 41)

I. Given three coplanar circles whose centers :g:`M_1 <0045.g1:M1>`, :g:`M_2 <0045.g1:M2>`, :g:`M_3 <0045.g1:M3>` are not collinear, each pair has an external and an internal center of similitude: :g:`A_3, I_3 <0045.g1:A3>` for :math:`(M_1, M_2)`, :g:`A_2, I_2 <0045.g1:A2>` for :math:`(M_1, M_3)`, and :g:`A_1, I_1 <0045.g1:A1>` for :math:`(M_2, M_3)`.

*Theorem (§ 13)*: **These six centers of similitude lie three by three along four straight lines**—the :term:`axes of similitude <aehnlichkeitsaxe>`:

1. One **external axis of similitude**: :g:`A_3 A_2 A_1 <0045.g1:/ A1 A3 />`.
2. Three **mixed axes of similitude**: :g:`A_3 I_2 I_1 <0045.g1:/ A3 I1 />`, :g:`A_2 I_1 I_3 <0045.g1:/ A2 I3 />`, and :g:`A_1 I_2 I_3 <0045.g1:/ A1 I2 />`.

Because parallel diameters can be drawn once a fixed circle is given, all six centers and four axes of similitude are straightedge-constructible!
