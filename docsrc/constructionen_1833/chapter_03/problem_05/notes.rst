Fifth Problem: Notes — Why Angle Trisection Still Fails
=======================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0059 (p. 55)

Angle bisection extracts quadratic cosines (:math:`\cos(\theta/2) = \sqrt{(1 + \cos\theta)/2}`), hence succeeds with straightedge + fixed circle; general angle trisection remains cubic and non-constructible in :math:`\mathbb{E}`.
