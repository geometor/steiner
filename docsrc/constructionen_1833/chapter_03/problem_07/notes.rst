Seventh Problem: Notes — Why Eichmaß Fails Where Fixed Circle Succeeds
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0062 (p. 58)

In 1899, :person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>` (:cite:`hilbert-1899`, Chapter VII, §§ 36–39) contrasted straightedge + :term:`Eichmaß <eichmass>` (which yields only the Pythagorean field closure :math:`\Omega_{\text{pyth}}`) with Steiner's drawn circle (Problem 7), which extracts arbitrary real square roots :math:`\sqrt{\alpha}` and thereby attains the full Euclidean quadratic closure :math:`\mathbb{E}` (:person:`Adler <adler>`, :cite:`adler-1890`).
