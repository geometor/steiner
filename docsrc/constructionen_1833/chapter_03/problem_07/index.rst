Seventh Problem (§ 18, [78]–[82]): Intersecting a Line with an Un-Drawn Circle
==============================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the mutual intersection points of a given straight line and a given (un-drawn, virtual) circle* :math:`(M_1, r_1)`. »

.. picture:: 0062.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 23 — Intersecting line g₁h₁ with un-drawn circle M₁ via homothety onto drawn fixed circle M (§ 18, Problem 7)
   :caption: Fig. 23 — Fixed circle M, virtual circle (M₁, a₁), similitude center A, and secants Ag, Ah yielding g₁, h₁ (§ 18, Problem 7)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0062, Fig. 23 (1895, p. 58)

   .. picture:: 0062.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 23 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 58)

Let :g:`g_1 h_1 <0062.g1:/ g1 h1 />` be the given line, and :g:`(M_1, a_1) <0062.g1:( M1 a1 )>` the un-drawn circle defined by its center :g:`M_1 <0062.g1:M1>` and radius segment :g:`M_1 a_1 <0062.g1:/ M1 a1 />`.

1. Construct the :term:`center of similitude <aehnlichkeitspunkte>` :g:`A <0062.g1:A>` (or :g:`I <0062.g1:I>`) of fixed circle :g:`M <0062.g1:( M a )>` and virtual circle :math:`(M_1, r_1)` via parallel radii :g:`Ma <0062.g1:/ a b />` and :g:`M_1 a_1 <0062.g1:/ M1 a1 />`.
2. Homothetically project line :g:`g_1 h_1 <0062.g1:/ g1 h1 />` from center :g:`A <0062.g1:A>` onto its corresponding line :g:`gh <0062.g1:/ g h />` relative to the **actually drawn fixed circle** :g:`M <0062.g1:( M a )>`, where :math:`gh \parallel g_1 h_1`.
3. Line :g:`gh <0062.g1:/ g h />` intersects the drawn circumference of :math:`\Omega` in two visible points :g:`g <0062.g1:g>` and :g:`h <0062.g1:h>`.
4. Draw straightedge rays :g:`Ag <0062.g1:/ A g1 />` and :g:`Ah <0062.g1:/ A h1 />`: their intersections with the given line are the exact intersection points :g:`g_1 <0062.g1:g1>` and :g:`h_1 <0062.g1:h1>` of the un-drawn circle with the line!
