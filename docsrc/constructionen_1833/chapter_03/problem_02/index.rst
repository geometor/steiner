Second Problem (§ 18, [71]): Segment Multiplication and Rational Division
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *Given a bounded segment on a straight line:*
  *a) To find another segment that is any given integer multiple of the first;*
  *b) To divide the given segment into any given number of equal parts;*
  *c) To construct a segment having any given rational ratio to the first.* »

By **Problem 1**, draw a straight line parallel to the given carrier line at an arbitrary distance. Because § 6 and § 8 in Chapter I already showed how to multiply any segment arbitrarily, divide a bounded segment into :math:`n` equal parts, and lay off proportional parts whenever **a parallel line is given**, all three parts (a), (b), (c) follow immediately by pure straightedge incidence (analogous to Euclid VI.9–10).
