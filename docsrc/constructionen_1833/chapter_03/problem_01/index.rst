First Problem (§ 18, [68]–[71]): Drawing a Parallel Through Any Given Point
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *Through any given point, to draw a straight line parallel to any given straight line.* »

.. picture:: 0055.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 19 — Straightedge parallel construction using fixed circle M and diameter ab (§ 18, Problem 1)
   :caption: Fig. 19 — Fixed circle M, diameter ab, given line ef, parallel chords cd and c₁ d₁, and constructed parallel hi (§ 18, Problem 1)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0055, Fig. 19 (1895, p. 51)

   .. picture:: 0055.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 19 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 51)

**Case a: When the given line passes through the center :math:`M` of the fixed circle.**
  The given line cuts the fixed circle :math:`\Omega` in a diameter :g:`ab <0055.g1:/ a b />` bisected at :g:`M <0055.g1:M>`. Using the complete quadrangle construction of Chapter I (§ 6), any external point is joined with :math:`a, M, b` to yield the exact fourth harmonic parallel at infinity in **6 straight lines**:

  .. math::

     \mathrm{Op.}_{\text{Parallel}} : 11R_1 + 6R_2, \qquad S = 17, \quad E = 11, \quad N_{\text{circles}} = 0.

**Case b: When the given line :g:`ef <0055.g1:/ e f />` does not pass through :math:`M`.**
  As illustrated in Fig. 19, draw any diameter :g:`ab <0055.g1:/ a b />` meeting :g:`ef <0055.g1:/ e f />` at :g:`g <0055.g1:g>`, pick any point :g:`c <0055.g1:c>` on :math:`\Omega`, and construct the symmetrical chords :g:`cd <0055.g1:/ c d />` and :g:`c_1 d_1 <0055.g1:/ c1 d1 />` via the intersections through :g:`M <0055.g1:M>`, transferring the given direction to a parallel diameter through :math:`M`, which reduces the construction through any external point to Case a along :g:`hi <0055.g1:/ h i />`.
