§ 19: Concluding Remark — The Indispensability of the Center M
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Concluding Remark (*Schlussbemerkung*, pp. 63–65 / ``[86]``–``[89]``):**

If only the continuous circle circumference :math:`\Omega` were drawn **without** its center :math:`M` being given, finding :math:`M`—or even drawing general parallel lines—by straightedge alone is **mathematically impossible**.

Any projective collineation of the plane preserving :math:`\Omega` (an automorphic collineation isomorphic to the hyperbolic isometry group of the disk) preserves all straight lines, collinearities, and harmonic cross-ratios, yet displaces the Euclidean center :math:`M` onto other interior points of :math:`\Omega`. Only the simultaneous presence of both the circumference and its center :math:`M` fixes the Euclidean line at infinity :math:`\ell_\infty` and enables complete geometric constructibility.
