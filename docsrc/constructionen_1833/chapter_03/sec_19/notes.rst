§ 19: Notes — Hyperbolic Collineations & Disk Automorphisms
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0067 (p. 63)

Steiner's Concluding Remark (§ 19, pp. 63–65, folios ``0067``–``0069``) stands as one of the earliest explicit projective impossibility proofs in synthetic geometry: the group :math:`\mathrm{PGL}(2,\mathbb{R})` acting as projective automorphisms of the conic :math:`\Omega` acts transitively on the interior open disk, making any un-marked interior point indistinguishable from :math:`M` under straightedge incidence alone. (In *Anhang* § 20 Note later, two intersecting circles or three given points do restore the metric via chords/bisectors.)
