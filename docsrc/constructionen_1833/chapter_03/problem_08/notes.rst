Eighth Problem: Notes — Completion of the Poncelet–Steiner Theorem
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0064 (p. 60)

Note that Steiner repeats Figure 18 verbatim on folio ``0065`` (p. 61) to remind the reader that when one of the circles is :math:`\Omega` itself, the anti-homologous cross-chords from similitude center :math:`A` yield the radical axis without computing squares or dropping auxiliary perpendiculars; Fig. 24 (folio ``0066``, p. 62) then generalizes to two arbitrary virtual circles :math:`M_1, M_2` mediated through :math:`M`.
