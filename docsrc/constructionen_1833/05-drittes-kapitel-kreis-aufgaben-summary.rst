========================================================================================================================
Summary: Reduction of Circle Intersections to Homothetic Projection
========================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: Quantitative Operations & Complete Reduction Schema

1. The Complete Reduction Schema
--------------------------------

Every Euclidean construction consists of a finite chain of three elementary intersection operations:

1. **Line–Line Meet**: Two lines intersect at a point :math:`P = L_1 \cap L_2` (directly drawn with straightedge).
2. **Line–Circle Meet**: A line intersects an un-drawn circle :math:`(M_1, r_1)` at points :math:`g_1, h_1` (solved by **Problem 7** via homothety to drawn circle :math:`\Omega`).
3. **Circle–Circle Meet**: Two un-drawn circles intersect at points :math:`P_1, P_2` (solved by **Problem 8** via radical axis, reducing directly to Problem 7).
