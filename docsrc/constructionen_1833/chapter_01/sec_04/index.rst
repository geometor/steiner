§ 4: The Complete Quadrilateral and Harmonic Diagonals
======================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

.. picture:: 0012.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 2 — Complete quadrilateral ACF GHI with harmonic diagonals AC, GF, HI (§ 4)
   :caption: Fig. 2 — Complete quadrilateral with six vertices A, C, F, G, H, I and three harmonic diagonals AC, GF, HI (§ 4)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0012, Fig. 2 (1895, p. 8)

   .. picture:: 0012.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 2 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 8)

Any four coplanar straight lines :math:`a, c, a_1, c_1`, no three concurrent, intersect pairwise in six vertices :g:`A <0012.g1:A>`, :g:`C <0012.g1:C>`, :g:`F <0012.g1:F>`, :g:`G <0012.g1:G>`, :g:`H <0012.g1:H>`, :g:`I <0012.g1:I>`, forming a :term:`complete quadrilateral <vollstaendiges-vierseit>` (*vollständiges Vierseit*).

Such a quadrilateral possesses three diagonals :g:`AC <0012.g1:/ A C />`, :g:`GF <0012.g1:/ G F />`, :g:`HI <0012.g1:/ H I />`, which intersect pairwise in the three diagonal points :g:`B <0012.g1:B>`, :g:`D <0012.g1:D>`, :g:`E <0012.g1:E>`.

*Theorem (§ 4)*: **In every complete quadrilateral, each of the three diagonals is cut harmonically by the other two**—that is, the points in which one diagonal meets the remaining two diagonals are harmonic conjugates with respect to the pair of vertices it joins (for example, :math:`A, B, C, D` are harmonic, and :math:`B, D` are conjugate harmonic points).
