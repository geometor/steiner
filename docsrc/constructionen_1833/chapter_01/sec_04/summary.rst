§ 4: Summary — Diagonal Harmony in the Complete Quadrilateral
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0012 (p. 8)

* **Diagonals (3)**: :math:`AC, GF, HI` intersecting in :math:`B = AC \cap HI`, :math:`D = AC \cap GF`, :math:`E = GF \cap HI`.
