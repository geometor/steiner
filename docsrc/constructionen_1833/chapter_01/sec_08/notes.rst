§ 8: Notes — Affine Geometry Without Metric Compass
===================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0021 (p. 17)

In modern terms, **§ 8** proves that fixing two non-parallel divided segments pins down the **line at infinity** :math:`\ell_\infty` projectively—turning the projective plane into a complete affine plane using straightedge incidence alone.
