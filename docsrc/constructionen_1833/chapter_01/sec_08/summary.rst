§ 8: Summary — Full Affine Completeness from Two Directions
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0021 (p. 17)

* **Two Independent Directions Suffice for Full Affine Geometry**: Once two non-parallel directions possess known midpoints/ratios (e.g., a single parallelogram :math:`ABCD`), every parallel line and every rational segment division anywhere in the plane becomes constructible with straightedge alone.
