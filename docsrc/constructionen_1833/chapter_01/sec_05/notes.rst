§ 5: Notes — Operation Count (S = 15) & Inaccessible Meets
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0013 (p. 9)

Note how Steiner immediately solves the **inaccessible intersection problem** (Task VI, p. 11, folio ``0015``) solely by projective incidence of the complete quadrilateral—a construction already crucial in military surveying and later used in Appendix Problem 22! Exact symbolic computation of Task I is implemented in :func:`geometor.steiner.constructions.steiner_harmonic_conjugate`.
