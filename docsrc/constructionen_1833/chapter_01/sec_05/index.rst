§ 5: Straightedge Construction of the Fourth Harmonic Point
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

From the harmonic diagonal theorem of **§ 4**, Steiner immediately derives six straightedge-only constructions (Tasks I–VI):

I. **Fourth Harmonic Point**: *Given three collinear points :math:`G, D, F`, to find the fourth harmonic point :math:`E` (conjugate to :math:`D`) using only a straightedge.*
   Choose any point :math:`A` off the line and draw rays :math:`AG, AD, AF`. Pick an arbitrary point :math:`C` on :math:`AD`, draw cross-joins :math:`GCI` (meeting :math:`AF` at :math:`I`) and :math:`FCH` (meeting :math:`AG` at :math:`H`); the line :math:`HI` intersects :math:`GF` in the required fourth harmonic point :math:`E`.

II. **Fourth Harmonic Ray**: *Given three concurrent rays :math:`a, b, c`, to construct the fourth harmonic ray :math:`d` conjugate to :math:`b` with the straightedge alone.*

III–IV. **Right Angles and Angle Bisectors**: *Given a right angle and one ray, to double the angle (construct the harmonic ray perpendicular to the arm); conversely, given three rays making two equal angles, to find the perpendicular harmonic fourth ray.*

V–VI. **Collinear Line Through an Inaccessible Intersection**: *Through a given point :math:`B`, to draw with the straightedge alone a line concurrent with two given lines whose intersection point :math:`F` is inaccessible due to obstacles.* (Compare *Abhäng. geom. Gestalten*, Vol. I, p. 77, :cite:`steiner-1832`.)
