§ 7: Notes — Continued-Fraction Reduction
=========================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0019 (p. 15)

Section **§ 7** (pp. 15–17, folios ``0019``–``0021``) establishes a purely projective form of the Euclidean algorithm: because harmonic cross-ratios depend only on rational ratios, any rational division ratio :math:`a : b` can be reduced to bisection (:math:`1 : 1`) with straightedge alone.
