========================================================================================================================
Notes & Commentary: Steiner's Creation of the "Power of a Point"
========================================================================================================================

.. rubric:: The Genesis of Radical Geometry in Berlin & Paris

1. Steiner's 1826 Coining of *Potenz des Punctes*
-------------------------------------------------

The concept of the :term:`power of a point <potenz-eines-punktes>` with respect to a circle is one of Jakob Steiner's most enduring gifts to geometry. First introduced in Crelle's *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* (Bd. 1, 1826, pp. 161–184) under the title *Einige geometrische Betrachtungen*, the power invariant :math:`d^2 - R^2` unified Euclid III.35 (intersecting chords) and III.36 (secant-tangent theorem) into a signed quadratic measure.

2. Gaultier, Chasles and the *Axe Radicale*
-------------------------------------------

Louis Gaultier (1813) had earlier defined the *axe radical*, but it was Steiner's systematic development that elevated it into an indispensable instrument for synthetic problem-solving. By realizing that the radical axis is the straight locus of equal powers, Steiner proved that two un-drawn circles can have their intersection locus constructed with a straightedge alone—bridging synthetic projective geometry and computational drafting.
