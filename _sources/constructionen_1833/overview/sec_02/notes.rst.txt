§ 2: Notes — From Mascheroni (1797) to Steiner (1833)
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0009 (p. 5)

Steiner acknowledges in **§ 2** (pp. 5–6) that while Mascheroni's compass-only constructions are of high value to mechanics and precision instrument makers, straightedge constructions with a fixed circle are often of even greater practical value to engineers and surveyors (*Ingenieurs und Feldmesser*). Note also that Steiner builds directly upon his 1832 *Systematische Entwickelung* (:cite:`steiner-1832`), while deliberately keeping the proofs in the 1833 treatise elementary so readers unfamiliar with projective geometry can follow every step.
