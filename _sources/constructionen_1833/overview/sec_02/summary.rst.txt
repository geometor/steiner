§ 2: Summary — Lineage of Minimal-Instrument Geometry
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0009 (p. 5)

* **1672 / 1797 — Compass Alone**: :person:`Georg Mohr <mohr>` (:cite:`mohr-1672`) and :person:`Lorenzo Mascheroni <mascheroni>` (:cite:`mascheroni-1797`) demonstrate that the straightedge is dispensable whenever straight lines are represented implicitly by pairs of points.
* **1759 / 1817 — Transversal & Perspective Precursors**: :person:`Lambert <lambert>` and :person:`Brianchon <brianchon>` construct parallels and harmonic points with straightedge alone given auxiliary figures.
* **1822 / 1833 — Straightedge + Fixed Circle**: :person:`Poncelet <poncelet>` states the fixed-circle principle; :person:`Steiner <steiner>` supplies a self-contained elementary proof in three chapters (rectilinear harmonic properties, circle similitude/power, and the eight fundamental problems).
