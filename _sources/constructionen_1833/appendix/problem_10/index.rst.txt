Appendix Problem 10: Line Cuts with Conic from 3 Points & 2 Tangents
====================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the mutual intersection points of a given straight line and a conic section of which (a) three points and two tangents, or (b) three tangents and two points are given.* »

Apply **Problem 6** (Case a) or **Problem 7** (Case b) to locate the missing contact points or tangents, yielding five elements for each of the (up to four) candidate conics, and cut with **Problem 2**.
