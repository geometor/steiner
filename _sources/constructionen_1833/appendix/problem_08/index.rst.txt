Appendix Problem 8: Line Cuts with Conic from 4 Points & 1 Tangent
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the mutual intersection points of a given straight line and a conic section of which (a) four points and one tangent, or (b) four tangents and one point are given.* »

By **Problem 4** (Case a) one finds the point of contact on the given tangent—providing five points on the conic—or by **Problem 5** (Case b) one obtains five tangents, reducing both cases immediately to **Problem 2**.
