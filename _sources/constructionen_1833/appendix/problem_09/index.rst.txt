Appendix Problem 9: Tangents from Point to Conic from 4 Tangents & 1 Point
==========================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the straight lines passing through a given point and touching a conic section of which (a) four tangents and one point, or (b) four points and one tangent are given.* »

Dual of Problem 8: invoke **Problem 5** (Case a) or **Problem 4** (Case b) to obtain five defining tangents or points, and then apply **Problem 3**.
