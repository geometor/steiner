Appendix Problem 15: Tangents from Point to Conic from 3 Tangents + 2 Contact Points
====================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the straight lines through a given point touching a conic given by (a) three tangents and the contact points of two of them, or (b) three points and the tangents at two of them.* »

Dual of Problem 14, reducing directly to **Problem 3**. Steiner adds (p. 74) that this composition rule immediately reveals the exact number of solutions (1, 2, or 4) across Problems 8–15!
