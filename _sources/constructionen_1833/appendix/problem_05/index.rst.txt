Appendix Problem 5: Tangent at a Point Given Four Tangents and One Point
========================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When four tangents and one point of a conic section are given, to find the tangent that touches the conic at that point.* »

Let :math:`A, B, C, D` be the four given tangents and :math:`\mathfrak{A}` the given point (p. 70 / ``[97]``). Mark the cuts of :math:`B, C, D` on :math:`A` (:math:`a, b, c`), and project the opposite vertices (:math:`D \cap C, D \cap B, C \cap B`) from :math:`\mathfrak{A}` onto :math:`A` at :math:`\alpha, \beta, \gamma`. The double points :math:`\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{s}` give the exact two tangents :math:`\mathfrak{Ar}, \mathfrak{As}` (dualizing Method I and II of Problem 4).
