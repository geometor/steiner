§ 20: Notes — Steiner's Cross-Axis Projective Solver
====================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0070 (p. 66)

Published immediately after *Systematische Entwickelung* (:cite:`steiner-1832`), § 20 (folios ``0070``–``0084``, pp. 66–80 / ``[90]``–``[110]``) showcases how a single drawn circle acts as an effective second-degree solver across projective conic geometry.
