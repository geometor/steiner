Appendix: Miscellaneous Conic & Surveying Constructions (§ 20 & Annotations)
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Anhang — Vermischte Aufgaben (§ 20, pp. 66–80 / [90]–[110]) & Anmerkungen (pp. 81–84)

The Appendix demonstrates the power of the fixed-circle straightedge method across 22 higher constructions: inscribed/circumscribed triangle loops (Problem 1), twenty paired projective-dual conic incidence, tangent, and Castillon–Poncelet polygon problems (Problems 2–21), and practical field surveying around terrain obstacles using staves and chain (Problem 22, Fig. 25), followed by A. J. von Oettingen's 1895 editorial annotations.
