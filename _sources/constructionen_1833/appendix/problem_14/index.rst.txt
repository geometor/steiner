Appendix Problem 14: Line Cuts with Conic from 3 Points + 2 Contact Tangents
============================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To find the mutual intersection points of a given straight line and a conic given by (a) three points together with the tangents at two of them, or (b) three tangents together with the contact points of two of them.* »

Giving three points and two tangents at two of them fixes a unique conic; **Problem 2** yields its line intersections.
