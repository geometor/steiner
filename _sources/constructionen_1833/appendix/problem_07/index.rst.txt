Appendix Problem 7: Tangents at Given Points Given Three Tangents and Two Points
================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When three tangents and two points of a conic section are given, to find the tangents at those two points.* »

Dually (pp. 71–72 / ``[99]``–``[100]``), join the two points :math:`\mathfrak{a}, \alpha` and construct the double pairs :math:`\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{s}` and :math:`\mathfrak{r}_1, \mathfrak{s}_1` induced by opposite tangent pairs :math:`(B, C)` and :math:`(B, D)` across :math:`\mathfrak{a}\alpha`. Crossing rays from triangle vertices :math:`\mathfrak{D}, \mathfrak{C}` locate centers :math:`u, x, y, z`, yielding **four** pairs of tangents tangent to four conics.
