Appendix Problem 17: Remaining Common Tangents & Points of Two Conics Sharing 2 Tangents
========================================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *When two conics :math:`K, K_1` have two common tangents :math:`A, A_1` given, together with any three further tangents :math:`a, b, c` and :math:`a_1, b_1, c_1` on each, to find their remaining two common tangents as well as their four common points.* »

Dualizing Problem 16 (p. 75 / ``[104]``), mark intersections on :math:`A` and :math:`A_1`, draw second tangents to :math:`K_1` from the intersections on :math:`A_1` meeting :math:`A` at :math:`\alpha, \beta, \gamma`, determine double points :math:`\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{s}` on :math:`A` via fixed circle :math:`\Omega`, and draw the second tangents from :math:`\mathfrak{r}, \mathfrak{s}` to :math:`K`—which touch both :math:`K` and :math:`K_1`! Dual pairs yield the four intersection points.
