2. Comparison with Classical Compass-and-Straightedge
-----------------------------------------------------

In classical geometry (:cite:`euclid-elements`), drawing a parallel requires Euclid I.31 (compass arcs to copy an angle, :math:`4R_1 + 2R_2 + 4C_1 + 3C_3, S = 13`). Steiner's parallel requires 4 additional ruler alignments (:math:`S = 17`), but completely eliminates all 3 compass arcs (:math:`m_1 = m_2 = m_3 = 0`).
