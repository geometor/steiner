Fourth Problem (§ 18, [75]): Laying Off an Angle Equal to a Given Angle
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *Through a given point, to draw a straight line making with a given straight line an angle equal to a given angle.* »

.. picture:: 0059.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 21 — Copying and bisecting angle AmC using central diameters ab and cd of fixed circle M (§ 18, Problems 4–5)
   :caption: Fig. 21 — Given angle AmC transferred to diameters ab, cd of M, bisecting chord angles onto pEq (§ 18, Problems 4–5)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0059, Fig. 21 (1895, p. 55)

   .. picture:: 0059.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 21 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 55)

Draw diameters :g:`ab <0059.g1:/ a b />` and :g:`cd <0059.g1:/ c d />` of the fixed circle :g:`M <0059.g1:M>` parallel (by Problem 1) to the two arms :g:`mA <0059.g1:/ m A />` and :g:`mC <0059.g1:/ m C />` of the given angle, transporting the angle directly to center :math:`M` as central angle :math:`\angle aMc = \angle AmC`. Drawing a parallel through the target point completes the transfer onto any target line :g:`pq <0059.g1:/ p q />`.
