§ 19: Summary — Why Center M Cannot Be Reconstructed
====================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0067 (p. 63)

* **Negative Theorem**: Given an empty circle :math:`\Omega` without its center :math:`M`, no finite straightedge construction can locate :math:`M`.
* **Minimal Condition**: One circle :math:`\Omega` **plus** its center :math:`M` (or any two intersecting circles, whose line of centers supplies centers by Problem 1/3) is both necessary and sufficient.
