Fifth Problem (§ 18, [76]): Bisecting and Multiplying a Given Angle
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *To bisect a given angle, or to multiply it by any integer.* »

Draw diameters :math:`ab, cd` of the fixed circle :math:`\Omega` parallel to the arms of the given angle (Problem 4), forming central angle :math:`\angle aMc`.

To **bisect** :math:`\angle aMc`, draw chord :math:`ac` (or :math:`ad` for the supplementary angle). In the isosceles triangle :math:`\triangle aMc`, the diameter through :math:`M` parallel to the third side :math:`ad` (or perpendicular to :math:`ac` by Problem 3) bisects :math:`\angle aMc` directly by straightedge alone! Iterating reflections across diameters multiplies the angle by any integer :math:`n`.
