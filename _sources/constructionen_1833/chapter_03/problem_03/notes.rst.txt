Third Problem: Notes — Orthogonal Elliptic Involution
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0056 (p. 52)

Projectively, the pairs of perpendicular chords through any point of :math:`\Omega` induce an elliptic orthogonal involution centered at :math:`M` (:cite:`lemoine-1902`, p. 75). While drafting uses set-squares (:term:`équerre <equerre>`), Steiner extracts exact orthogonality from pure incidence and Thales' theorem.
