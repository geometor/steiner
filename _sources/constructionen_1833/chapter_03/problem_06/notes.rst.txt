Sixth Problem: Notes — Virtual Circles (M₁, a₁)
===============================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0060 (p. 56)

Notice that while Problem 6 solves *transferring* a segment along a straight ray—analogous to Hilbert's *Eichmaß* (:term:`Eichmaß <eichmass>`, :cite:`hilbert-1899`)—only the continuously drawn circle circumference :math:`\Omega` can supply the *intersections* of Problems 7 and 8.
