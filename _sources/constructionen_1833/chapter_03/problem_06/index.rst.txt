Sixth Problem (§ 18, [76]–[78]): Transferring a Given Segment
=============================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

« *From a given point along a given ray, to lay off a segment equal in length to a given segment.* »

.. picture:: 0060.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 22 — Transferring segment M₁a₁ onto rays from M₂ via fixed circle M and internal similitude centers I₁, I₂ (§ 18, Problem 6)
   :caption: Fig. 22 — Fixed circle M, diameter ab || M₁a₁, destination center M₂, and similitude centers I₁, I₂ (§ 18, Problem 6)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0060, Fig. 22 (1895, p. 56)

   .. picture:: 0060.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 22 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 56)

Given a segment :g:`M_1 a_1 <0060.g1:/ M1 a1 />`, we regard it as the radius of an un-drawn (virtual) circle :math:`(M_1, r_1)`. To lay off this exact length from a new point :g:`M_2 <0060.g1:M2>` along any ray:

1. Through center :g:`M <0060.g1:M>` of the fixed circle :g:`(Ma) <0060.g1:( M a )>`, draw diameter :g:`ab <0060.g1:/ a b />` parallel to :g:`M_1 a_1 <0060.g1:/ M1 a1 />` (by Problem 1).
2. Line :math:`ba_1` intersects :g:`MM_1 <0060.g1:/ M M1 />` at the internal :term:`center of similitude <aehnlichkeitspunkte>` :g:`I_1 <0060.g1:I1>` (or :math:`aa_1` gives the external center :math:`A_1`).
3. Transferring center :math:`M_1` to target :g:`M_2 <0060.g1:M2>` along equal parallel radii via :g:`I_2 <0060.g1:I2>` projects radius :math:`R` back onto any desired direction around :g:`M_2 <0060.g1:M2>` in exact equal length :math:`M_1 a_1`, using straightedge alone!
