§ 6: Constructions with Given Parallels or Bisected Segments
============================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Part II.A — Constructions Using Straightedge Alone When Parallels or Rationally Divided Segments Are Given.**

When, in Task I (§ 5), the three collinear points :g:`G, D, F <0016.g1:G>` satisfy the special condition that :g:`D <0016.g1:D>` is the **midpoint** of :g:`GF <0016.g1:/ G F />`, the fourth harmonic point :math:`E` recedes to infinity, so line :g:`HI <0016.g1:/ I H />` is strictly **parallel** to :g:`GF <0016.g1:/ G F />`.

.. picture:: 0016.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 3 — Parallel construction via bisected segment GF and midpoint D (§ 6)
   :caption: Fig. 3 — Triangle GAF with midpoint D of GF and parallel HI || GF (§ 6, I–III)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0016, Fig. 3 (1895, p. 12)

   .. picture:: 0016.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 3 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 12)

From this single observation follow four fundamental affine constructions:

* **I. Parallel from a Bisected Segment**: Given :g:`G, D, F <0016.g1:D>` with :eq:`GD = DF <0016.g1:/ F D / = 0016.g1:/ D G />`, draw :g:`GH, FH <0016.g1:/ F H />`, pick :g:`A <0016.g1:A>` on :g:`GH`, draw :g:`AD <0016.g1:/ A D />` and :g:`AF <0016.g1:/ A F />`; through :g:`C = FH \cap AD <0016.g1:C>` draw :g:`GCI <0016.g1:/ G I />` meeting :g:`AF` at :g:`I <0016.g1:I>`. Then :g:`HI <0016.g1:/ I H />` is parallel to :g:`GF <0016.g1:/ F G />`.
* **II. Bisecting Any Segment on Given Parallels**: Given two parallels :g:`GF, HI <0016.g1:/ I H />`, join any point :g:`A` to :g:`G, F` cutting :g:`HI` at :g:`H, I`; cross-joins :g:`HF, IG` meet at :g:`C`; line :g:`AC` bisects :g:`GF` at :g:`D`.
* **III. Third Parallel Through Any Point**: Bisect any segment on one of two given parallels by (II), then apply (I).
* **IV. Segment Multiplication and Rational Division**: Using three parallels (:g:`HA, bf, BZ <0017.g1:/ B Z />`), repeated harmonic projections construct :math:`n`-fold multiples (:g:`BC, CD, DE, EF <0017.g1:/ B C />`) or reciprocals :math:`\frac{1}{2}CB, \frac{1}{3}CB, \frac{1}{4}CB, \dots` (:g:`C, G, F, E, D, B <0019.g1:/ C B />`, a method credited to :person:`Brianchon <brianchon>`, :cite:`brianchon-1817`, p. 37).
