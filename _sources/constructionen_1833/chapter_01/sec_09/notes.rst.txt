§ 9: Notes — Why a Square Still Cannot Extract Square Roots
===========================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0024 (p. 20)

Section **§ 9** (pp. 20–22, folios ``0024``–``0026``) completes Chapter I. Notice the mathematical crescendo: a parallelogram gives affine geometry; a square adds right angles and perpendicularity; yet only the **drawn circle** in Chapter II & III unlocks quadratic irrationalities (:math:`\mathbb{E}`).
