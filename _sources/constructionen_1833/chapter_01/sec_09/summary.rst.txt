§ 9: Summary — Orthogonal Involution from a Square
==================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0024 (p. 20)

* **Affine + Orthogonal**: A given square :math:`ABCD` supplies both :math:`\ell_\infty` (via parallels) and the circular points / right-angle involution (via :math:`\triangle EBI \cong \triangle ECF`).
* **Limitation**: A straightedge plus a square can construct all linear and orthogonal operations (rational coordinates :math:`\mathbb{Q}`), but **cannot** extract general square roots or transfer segments of arbitrary direction without a drawn circle!
