§ 9: Perpendiculars and Angle Operations from a Given Square
============================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Part II.C — When a Square Is Given.**

.. picture:: 0025.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 11 — Constructing perpendiculars and bisecting right angles from square ABCD (§ 9)
   :caption: Fig. 11 — Square ABCD with center E, perpendicular lines FEG and IEK, and external perpendicular (§ 9)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0025, Fig. 11 (1895, p. 21)

   .. picture:: 0025.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 11 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 21)

Besides all affine constructions obtainable from a parallelogram (§ 8, I), when the parallelogram is specifically a **square** :g:`ABCD <0025.g1:< A B C D >>` with center :g:`E = AC \cap BD <0025.g1:E>`, one can also with the straightedge alone:

a) **Drop a perpendicular** onto any given line :g:`gf <0025.g1:/ g f />` from any given point :g:`i <0025.g1:i>`: draw :g:`GEF <0025.g1:/ G F />` through :g:`E` parallel to :g:`gf`; draw :g:`FH <0025.g1:/ H F />` parallel to :g:`BC` meeting :g:`AB` at :g:`H`, then :g:`HI <0025.g1:/ H I />` parallel to diagonal :g:`AC` meeting :g:`BC` at :g:`I <0025.g1:I>`. Since :math:`\triangle EBI \cong \triangle ECF`, :math:`\angle IEF = \angle BEC = 90^\circ`, so a parallel to :g:`IEK <0025.g1:/ I K />` through :g:`i` is the required perpendicular!
b) **Bisect any given right angle** (via isosceles right triangle :math:`\triangle IEF` and perpendicular :g:`EL <0025.g1:/ E L />` to :math:`IF`).
c) **Multiply any given angle** by any integer :math:`n` (repeatedly applying Problem (a) and the harmonic angle-doubling property of § 5, III).
