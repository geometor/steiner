§ 3: Summary — Cross-Ratio Invariance & Midpoint Criterion
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0010 (p. 6)

* **Harmonic Cross-Ratio**: :math:`(A, B; C, D) = \frac{AC}{BC} : \frac{AD}{BD} = -1 \iff \frac{2}{AB} = \frac{1}{AC} + \frac{1}{AD}`.
* **Parallel Criterion (Midpoint-Infinity Duality)**: When ray :math:`d` is parallel to base :math:`AC`, the fourth harmonic point recedes to infinity and :math:`b` bisects :math:`AC`.
* **Orthogonal Bisector Criterion**: Two conjugate rays :math:`b \perp d` are perpendicular if and only if they bisect the angle between :math:`a, c` and its supplement.
