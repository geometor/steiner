§ 3: Harmonic Rays and Harmonic Point Ranges
============================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

.. picture:: 0010.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 1 — Harmonic pencil B(A, b, C, d) and transversal abcd (§ 3)
   :caption: Fig. 1 — Harmonic rays a, b, c, d from vertex B and transversal abcd (§ 3)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0010, Fig. 1 (1895, p. 6)

   .. picture:: 0010.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 1 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 6)

I. Let :g:`ABC <0010.g1:< A B C >>` be any triangle: from vertex :g:`B <0010.g1:B>`, let ray :g:`Bb <0010.g1:/ B b />` pass through the midpoint :g:`b <0010.g1:b>` of base :g:`AC <0010.g1:/ A C />`, and let ray :g:`Bd <0010.g1:/ B d />` be parallel to base :g:`AC <0010.g1:/ A C />`. If any transversal straight line :g:`ad <0010.g1:/ a d />` is drawn through midpoint :g:`b <0010.g1:b>`, cutting the sides :g:`BA, BC` and rays :g:`Bb, Bd` in four points :g:`a <0010.g1:a>`, :g:`b <0010.g1:b>`, :g:`c <0010.g1:c>`, :g:`d <0010.g1:d>`, then by similar triangles :math:`\triangle aAb \sim \triangle aBd` and :math:`\triangle cCb \sim \triangle cBd`, since :eq:`Ab = Cb <0010.g1:/ A b / = 0010.g1:/ b C />`:

.. math::

   \frac{ab}{ad} = \frac{cb}{cd} \quad \text{or} \quad ab : bc = ad : cd.

Four concurrent straight lines :math:`a, b, c, d` emanating from a common vertex :g:`B <0010.g1:B>` form a :term:`harmonic pencil of rays <harmonische-strahlen>` if every transversal intersecting them in four points :math:`A, B, C, D` produces a :term:`harmonic range <harmonische-punkte>`, defined by the invariant cross-ratio:

.. math::

   (A, B; C, D) = \frac{AC}{BC} : \frac{AD}{BD} = -1.

II–IV. Every transversal cutting four harmonic rays cuts them in four harmonic points; conversely, four rays joining any point to four harmonic points form a harmonic pencil. When :math:`D` recedes to infinity (:math:`Bd \parallel AC`), :math:`C` is the exact **midpoint** of :g:`AB <0010.g1:/ A C />`. Moreover, if two conjugate harmonic rays :math:`b, d` are perpendicular to each other, they bisect the interior and supplementary exterior angles between :math:`a` and :math:`c`.
