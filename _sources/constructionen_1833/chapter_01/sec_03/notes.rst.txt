§ 3: Notes — Projective Invariance of Harmonic Pencils
======================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0010 (p. 6)

Section **§ 3** (pp. 6–8, folios ``0010``–``0012``) lays down the two affine bridges inside projective geometry that power Steiner's entire book: (1) harmonic conjugation with a point at infinity gives the **midpoint** of a segment (yielding **parallels** in § 6 and Problem 1), and (2) orthogonal conjugate harmonic rays give **angle bisectors** and **right angles** (yielding Problems 3–5).
