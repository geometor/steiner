§ 8: Constructions from a Parallelogram or Two Rational Ratios
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Part II.B — When Two Pairs of Parallels (a Parallelogram) or Two Rationally Divided Segments Are Given.**

I. *Given any parallelogram :g:`ABCD <0022.g1:< A B C D >>` in the plane, with diagonals :g:`AC <0022.g1:/ A C />` and :g:`BD <0022.g1:/ B D />` meeting at :g:`E <0022.g1:E>`, one can draw with the straightedge alone:* (a) a line parallel to any given line in any direction through any given point, and (b) any rational multiple or subdivision of any segment.

.. picture:: 0022.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 7 — Constructing parallels in arbitrary direction from parallelogram ABCD (§ 8, I)
   :caption: Fig. 7 — Parallelogram ABCD with center parallel EF bisecting any transversal GK (§ 8, I)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0022, Fig. 7 (1895, p. 18)

   .. picture:: 0022.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 7 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 18)

Indeed, drawing :g:`EF <0022.g1:/ E F />` through :g:`E` parallel to :g:`AD, BC` creates three equidistant parallels (:g:`AD, EF, BC`) which bisect any non-parallel transversal :g:`GK <0022.g1:/ G K />` at :g:`F <0022.g1:F>`, immediately enabling parallels to :g:`GK` via § 6, I!

II. The exact same universal affine power holds whenever any of the following four data are given: (a) three parallels cutting a fourth line in a given rational ratio (Fig. 8), (b) two parallels bearing two segments in a given rational ratio (Fig. 8), (c) two parallels and one divided segment (Fig. 9), or (d) two non-parallel divided segments (Fig. 10).
