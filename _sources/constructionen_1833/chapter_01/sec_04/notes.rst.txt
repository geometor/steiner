§ 4: Notes — Lazare Carnot (1803) & Pappus Book VII
===================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0012 (p. 8)

As :person:`A. J. von Oettingen <oettingen>` remarks in his editorial notes (1895, p. 84, Note 2), the terms *vollständiges Vierseit* (complete quadrilateral) and *vollständiges Viereck* (complete quadrangle) were introduced by Lazare Nicolas Marguerite Carnot in *Géométrie de position* (1803), generalizing the harmonic lemma preserved by :person:`Pappus of Alexandria <pappus>` in *Synagoge* Book VII (:cite:`pappus-synagoge`).
