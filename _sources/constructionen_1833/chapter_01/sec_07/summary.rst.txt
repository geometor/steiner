§ 7: Summary — Euclidean Descent to Ratio 1 : 1
===============================================

.. rubric:: Structural & Geometrographic Summary — Folio 0019 (p. 15)

* **Harmonic Ratio Identity**: If :math:`(B, D; C, E) = -1` and :math:`BD : DC = a : b`, then :math:`CE = \frac{b(a+b)}{a-b}` and :math:`BC : CE = (a - b) : b`.
* **Termination**: Repeated harmonic conjugation implements the Euclidean algorithm on :math:`(a, b)`, reaching :math:`1 : 1` in finitely many steps (e.g., 1 step for :math:`a=2, b=1` or :math:`a=3, b=1`).
