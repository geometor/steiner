§ 10: Harmonic Properties, Poles, and Polars of the Circle
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Chapter II — Part I: Harmonic Properties of the Circle.**

I. Let :g:`a, b, c, d <0027.g1:a>` be four harmonic points on a line, and let :g:`B <0027.g1:B>` be a point such that the conjugate rays :g:`Ba <0027.g1:/ B a />` and :g:`Bc <0027.g1:/ B c />` are perpendicular; by § 3.IV they bisect the angles between :g:`Bb` and :g:`Bd`, and the locus of :g:`B` is the circle :g:`M <0027.g1:( M a )>` on diameter :g:`ac <0027.g1:/ a c />`.

II–IV. If any secant drawn through a fixed pole :g:`P <0027.g1:P>` cuts a given circle :g:`M <0027.g1:( M a )>` in points :g:`a <0027.g1:a>` and :g:`c <0027.g1:c>`, and on this secant the fourth harmonic point :g:`b <0027.g1:b>` is determined such that :math:`(a, c; b, P) = -1`, the locus of all such conjugate points :math:`b` as the secant rotates is a single straight line: the :term:`polar <pol-und-polare>` :g:`pq <0027.g1:/ p q />` of :g:`P` with respect to the circle.

Because the polar relation is purely harmonic, the straightedge alone constructs it for any point :math:`P`:

1. Draw two secants through :math:`P` cutting the circle at :math:`A, B` and :math:`C, D`.
2. Join the cross-chords: :math:`AD \cap BC = E` and :math:`AC \cap BD = F`.
3. Line :math:`EF` is the polar line :g:`pq <0027.g1:/ p q />` of :math:`P`!

If :g:`P <0027.g1:P>` lies outside the circle, its polar :g:`pq <0027.g1:/ p q />` cuts the circumference in the exact points of contact :g:`p, q <0027.g1:p>` of the two tangents :g:`dp, dq <0027.g1:/ d p />` from :g:`P`—allowing tangents to any drawn circle to be constructed with a straightedge alone in :math:`16R_1 + 9R_2` (:math:`S = 25`, zero circles)!
