§ 12: Notes — Steiner's Footnote on the Nine-Point Circle
=========================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0036 (p. 32)

Oettingen remarks (1895, p. 84, Note 4) that the circle :math:`M_1` in Steiner's remarkable footnote (pp. 37–40, folios ``0041``–``0044``) is now universally called **Feuerbach's circle** (*Feuerbach'scher Kreis*, nine-point circle, discovered by Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach in 1822). Exact computation of :math:`(A, I)` is provided in :func:`geometor.steiner.constructions.steiner_centers_of_similitude`.
