§ 17: Common Power and Straightedge Radical Axis via Similitude
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

**Part III.B — On Common Power (*Gemeinschaftliche Potenz*).**

.. picture:: 0051.g1.svg
   :alt: Figure 18 — Common power and radical axis rs via center of similitude A (§ 17)
   :caption: Fig. 18 — Circles M, M1 with center of similitude A, secant rays, anti-homologous pairs, and radical axis rs (§ 17)

.. collapse:: Archival Plate — Folio 0051, Fig. 18 (1895, p. 47)

   .. picture:: 0051.g1.png
      :alt: Archival scan of Fig. 18 from Ostwald's Klassiker Nr. 60 (p. 47)

I. Draw any secant from the external center of similitude :g:`A <0051.g1:A>` of two circles :g:`M <0051.g1:( M a )>` and :g:`M_1 <0051.g1:( M1 a1 )>`, cutting them in :g:`a, b <0051.g1:a>` and :g:`a_1, b_1 <0051.g1:a1>`. While :math:`(a, a_1)` and :math:`(b, b_1)` are homologous points, the cross-pairs :math:`(a, b_1)` and :math:`(b, a_1)` are **anti-homologous** (*potenzhaltende Punkte*), satisfying the constant **common power**:

.. math::

   Aa \cdot Ab_1 = Ab \cdot Aa_1 = Ac \cdot Ad_1 = \text{const.}

II. Consequently, for any two secants through :g:`A <0051.g1:A>`, the four points :g:`a, b_1, c, d_1 <0051.g1:a>` lie on a circle, and the chords :g:`ad_1 <0051.g1:/ a d1 />` and :g:`cb_1 <0051.g1:/ c b1 />` (and likewise opposite pairs of cross-chords) intersect directly on the **radical axis** :g:`rs <0051.g1:/ r s />` of circles :math:`M` and :math:`M_1`!

This allows the radical axis of two circles to be constructed **with straightedge alone** from their centers of similitude and a few secant chords—without drawing any auxiliary circle!
