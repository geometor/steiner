§ 14: Notes — Euclidean Orthogonal Decomposition
================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0047 (p. 43)

Section **§ 14** (pp. 43–44, folios ``0047``–``0048``) isolates the pure quadratic-difference lemma so that **§ 15** can immediately identify :math:`u^2 = R^2 - R_1^2` as the radical axis of two circles.
