§ 16: Notes — Radical Center & Common Chord Theorem
===================================================

.. rubric:: Historical, Algebraic & Archival Apparatus — Folio 0050 (p. 46)

Section **§ 16** (pp. 46–47, folios ``0050``–``0051``) supplies the exact concurrency lemma behind the classical straightedge construction of the radical axis of two non-intersecting circles (intersecting both with an auxiliary circle and dropping the perpendicular from their radical center).
