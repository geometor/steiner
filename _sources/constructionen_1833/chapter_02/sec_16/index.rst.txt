§ 16: The Radical Center of Three Circles
=========================================

.. rubric:: Jakob Steiner — *Die geometrischen Constructionen* (Berlin, 1833 / Leipzig, 1895)

Consider three coplanar circles :math:`M_1, M_2, M_3` whose centers are not collinear, with pairwise radical axes :math:`N_3 Q_3`, :math:`N_2 Q_2`, and :math:`N_1 Q_1`.

Let :math:`q` be the intersection of :math:`N_3 Q_3` and :math:`N_2 Q_2`. Since :math:`q` has equal power with respect to :math:`M_1, M_2` and equal power with respect to :math:`M_1, M_3`, it necessarily has equal power with respect to :math:`M_2, M_3` and therefore lies on the third radical axis :math:`N_1 Q_1`.

This unique concurrency point :math:`q` is the :term:`point of equal powers <punkt-der-gleichen-potenzen>` (**radical center**). Consequently:

* **(a)** The three radical axes of three circles concur in one point :math:`q`.
* **(b)** If three circles intersect pairwise, their three common secant chords concur in :math:`q`.
* **(c)** If three circles are mutually tangent, their three common tangents at the points of contact concur in :math:`q`.
